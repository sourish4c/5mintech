How Five Minutes Tech handles your data — what I collect, what I don't, and how to reach me about it.

---

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025

I respect your privacy and am committed to handling any data that passes through this site with care. This page explains exactly what I collect, why, and the choices you have.

### Information I Collect

- **Basic analytics**: aggregated page views, referrer, browser type, and country (city-level, never precise location). Used only to understand which tutorials are useful and where to focus next.
- **Voluntary data**: anything you send through the contact form or email me directly. I never sell or share it.
- **Server logs**: standard request logs retained for up to 30 days for abuse detection and debugging, then deleted.

### How I Use Your Information

- To improve tutorials based on which posts get read to the end and which bounce.
- To respond when you reach out via the contact form or email.
- To detect and block spam, scraping, or abuse.

### Cookies

The site uses only strictly-necessary cookies (for session state and theme preference) and, if enabled, a privacy-respecting analytics tool that does not set advertising cookies. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time without losing access to the content.

### Third-Party Services

The site is hosted on **Cloudflare Pages**. Cloudflare may process requests through its global edge network, which is a necessary part of serving the site to you. Cloudflare's privacy practices are documented at [cloudflare.com/privacypolicy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

If I add a third-party analytics, comments, or newsletter service in the future, I will:
1. Pick a privacy-respecting vendor (no advertising trackers, no data resale).
2. Update this page to name them and link to their privacy policy.
3. Add a cookie banner if the service is not strictly necessary.

I do not embed advertising trackers, Facebook pixels, or any ad-network scripts on this site.

### Contact Form Processor

The contact form on this site is processed by **Web3Forms**, a third-party form-handling service. When you submit the form, the data you enter (name, email, phone if provided, and your message) is sent to Web3Forms over HTTPS, which then forwards it to my email inbox.

What Web3Forms does and doesn't do with your data:

- It receives the form payload and relays it to my inbox. It is **not** used for marketing, advertising, model training, or sold to third parties.
- Submissions are stored on Web3Forms' infrastructure only as long as needed to deliver the email. Their full data handling practices are documented at [web3forms.com/privacy](https://web3forms.com/privacy).
- The form includes a hidden bot-check field; automated submissions that trip the check are rejected before they reach me or Web3Forms' storage.

If you'd rather not use the form, you can email me directly at [blog@sourish4c.com](mailto:blog@sourish4c.com).

### Data Hosting and International Transfers

The site is hosted on Cloudflare's edge network, which serves cached content from data centers in multiple countries. Your requests may be processed in a region different from your own. Cloudflare's data handling is described in their privacy policy linked above.

### Data Retention

- Server logs: up to 30 days.
- Contact-form submissions: deleted from my inbox once the conversation is closed and any agreed action is complete.
- Email correspondence: retained as long as it's useful for our ongoing communication, then deleted on request.

### Your Rights

You can ask me at any time to:
- Confirm whether I hold any data about you.
- Provide a copy of that data.
- Delete the data.

Send requests to [blog@sourish4c.com](mailto:blog@sourish4c.com) and I'll respond within 30 days.

### Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect any data from anyone in that age group. If you believe a child has submitted data through the contact form, email me and I'll delete it.

### Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The effective date at the top of this page reflects the most recent change. Continued use of this site after an update implies acceptance of the revised version. Material changes will be noted at the top of the post for at least 30 days.
