### Welcome to Five Minutes Tech

We believe learning doesn’t have to be overwhelming or time-consuming. That’s why every tutorial here is designed to be read in under 5 minutes, giving you just the essentials you need to learn, build, and apply.

### Topics we cover

* AI & Chatbots
* Cloud and DevOps (AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, Jenkins)
* Python programming
* Linux, Bash, and Home Lab setups

Whether you're a beginner or an experienced engineer, you’ll find tutorials that get straight to the point — **no fluff, just results.**

### Why Five Minutes Tech?

* Bite-sized tutorials – learn something useful in 5 minutes.
* Practical focus – no fluff, just hands-on steps.
* Wide coverage – AI, DevOps, cloud, and beyond.
* For busy people – perfect for learners who don’t have hours to spare.

### About Me

Hi, I’m Sourish, the creator of Five Minutes Tech. I’m passionate about simplifying complex technology and breaking it down into digestible, easy-to-follow lessons. Over the years, I’ve worked with AI, DevOps tools, cloud platforms, and automation frameworks, and I know how tough it can be to keep up with everything. That’s why I built this platform – to make learning fast, approachable, and practical.

When I’m not writing tutorials, you’ll probably find me experimenting with home labs, exploring new AI tools, or contributing to open-source projects.

Want a sample of the writing? Browse the [latest tutorials](/posts/) — each one ships with copy-pasteable commands and a working end-to-end example.
