#### Welcome to Five Minutes Tech (5min.tech) – your go-to destination for quick, practical tech tutorials.

We believe learning doesn’t have to be overwhelming or time-consuming. That’s why every tutorial here is designed to be read in under 5 minutes, giving you just the essentials you need to learn, build, and apply.

#### We cover a wide range of topics like:

* AI & Chatbots
* Cloud and DevOps (AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, Jenkins)
* Python programming
* Linux, Bash, and Home Lab setups

Whether you're a beginner or an experienced engineer, you’ll find tutorials that get straight to the point — **no fluff, just results.**

#### Why Five Minutes Tech?

* Bite-sized tutorials – learn something useful in 5 minutes.
* Practical focus – no fluff, just hands-on steps.
* Wide coverage – AI, DevOps, cloud, and beyond.
* For busy people – perfect for learners who don’t have hours to spare.
